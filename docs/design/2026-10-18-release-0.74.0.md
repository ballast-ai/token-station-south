## 1. What this release carries

- **#272, family model parameters.** The host's dialect knowledge moves into manifests:
  - the provider-world field `model_parameters` declares, per family, the model-parameter words the package reads
    (`words`) and the sets of words a model declares at most one of (`exclusive`). The host derives its reserved
    namespaces and admitted words from the loaded packages instead of importing south constants by name;
  - the provider-world field `drops_unsigned_thinking` names the families whose upstream refuses unsigned thinking on
    the Responses surface (only `anthropic`, MP-Q1 and MP-Q2);
  - `provider-anthropic` protects `anthropic-version` on family `anthropic` with `immutable_request_headers`;
  - every provider family refuses a forced tool choice itself (lv, 2026-10-18: no family is exempt by its wire), so
    the host can delete its pre-refusal after it re-pins.
- **#272, section-level credential attributes and the task-v2 value channel.**
  - `CredentialsV1.attributes` exports a stored non-secret field whatever its slot's source, under a complete
    recipe's attribute rules; a section attribute may not share a name with a recipe attribute.
  - `TASK_WORLD_SCHEMA_V2.value_channel` is `true`: a task-v2 package may export credential attributes, which reach
    it through `ProviderConfig.declared`.
  - `task-gmi-image-v2` 0.36.0 declares a `credentials` section (`api_key`, and an optional `organization_id` exported
    as a section attribute) and reads the organization id from `declared`, falling back to
    `extensions.organization_id` (SA-Q3).

## 2. API changes

- `south-provider-api` 0.61.0 → 0.62.0: `ComponentManifestV1` gains `model_parameters` and
  `drops_unsigned_thinking`; `ModelParametersV1`, `MAX_MODEL_PARAMETERS`, `MAX_MODEL_PARAMETER_BYTES` and
  `MAX_MODEL_PARAMETER_GROUPS` are new; `ManifestErrorV1` gains `InvalidModelParameters` and
  `InvalidDropsUnsignedThinkingFamily`; `CredentialsV1` gains `attributes`, and `attribute_names` and
  `admits_attribute` include section attributes; the task-v2 world schema admits the value channel.
- `south-component-conformance` 0.72.0 → 0.73.0: `declared_parameters::family` (each shipped family's words and the
  Claude exclusive pair), `exported_attributes_v1` exports section attributes for every slot, the forced-tool refusal
  in the `kiro`, OpenAI-compatible, Gemini, Vertex and OpenAI Responses references, and the GMI reference reading
  `declared`.
- `south-contracts` (0.56.0) is unchanged. No contract number changes; the kernel pin is unchanged.

Unverified: the upstream behaviour the records describe (nothing was called against an upstream).
